## What is a Threat Actor?
A threat actor is an individual or group that carries out cyberattacks against systems, networks, or organizations. They may work independently or as part of an organized team with specialized skills.
## Types of Threat Actors
- Organized Groups: Teams with members specializing in programming, network exploitation, social engineering, data analysis, and coordinating attacks.
- Solo Actors: Individuals who operate independently and may have varying levels of skill, resources, and experience.
## Common Techniques
Threat actors may use reconnaissance to identify weaknesses, exploit vulnerabilities to gain access, and extract data while attempting to avoid detection. Some use low-and-slow techniques, performing actions gradually to avoid triggering security alerts.
## Objectives and Motivations
- Financial Gain: Stealing money or selling stolen information.
- Espionage: Collecting confidential information for strategic advantage.
- Disruption: Disabling services, deleting data, or spreading misinformation.
- Ideology: Promoting political, religious, or social causes.
- Revenge: Retaliating against an organization or individual.
## Key Takeaway

Threat actors can be individuals or organized groups with different skills, resources, and motivations. Understanding their objectives and techniques helps organizations anticipate threats and improve their defenses.
