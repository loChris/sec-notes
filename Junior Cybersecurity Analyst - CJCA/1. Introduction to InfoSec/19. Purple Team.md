## What is a Purple Team?
A Purple Team approach combines the strengths of the Red Team (offense) and the Blue Team (defense) through collaboration and knowledge sharing.

Rather than working separately, both teams work together to understand attacks, improve defenses, and strengthen an organization's overall security.
## Purpose
Purple Teaming aims to:
- Improve Security Defenses: Red Teams share attack techniques so Blue Teams can strengthen their protections.
- Enhance Detection and Response: Blue Teams help identify ways to improve attack detection and response.
- Encourage Continuous Improvement: Both teams adapt their strategies as new threats emerge.
## Key Objectives
- Collaborative Security Testing: Simulate attacks while defenders monitor and respond in real time.
- Knowledge Sharing: Exchange offensive and defensive techniques to develop skills and understanding.
- Continuous Monitoring and Adaptation: Keep security strategies updated as threats evolve.
- Enhanced Incident Response: Improve coordination and prepare both teams to respond more effectively to real attacks.
## Key Takeaway
Purple Teaming is a collaborative approach that connects offense and defense. By sharing knowledge and testing security together, Red and Blue Teams can identify weaknesses, improve detection, and build stronger defenses.
