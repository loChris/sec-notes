## What is a CISO?
A CISO is a senior executive responsible for an organization's overall cybersecurity strategy. They oversee the protection of sensitive information, technology, and digital assets while ensuring security supports the organization's business goals.
## Key Responsibilities
- Security Strategy: Develop security programs, policies, and procedures to protect systems and data.
- Risk Management: Identify vulnerabilities, assess risks, and determine how to reduce or manage them.
- Incident Response: Coordinate response efforts to minimize damage and restore normal operations after security incidents.
- Policies and Compliance: Establish security standards and ensure the organization follows relevant regulations.
- Security Technology: Oversee tools and controls used to defend the organization.
- Collaboration: Work with executives and other departments to balance security requirements with business needs.
- Security Awareness: Promote a culture where employees understand their role in protecting information.
## Purpose
The CISO protects the confidentiality, integrity, and availability (CIA triad) of an organization's information while helping the business operate effectively.

Their work includes reviewing security reports, overseeing risk assessments, coordinating incident responses, meeting with executives, and staying informed about emerging threats.
## Key Takeaway
The CISO is responsible for the organization's overall cybersecurity direction and risk management. Unlike analysts and engineers who may handle specific technical tasks, the CISO focuses on strategy, leadership, coordination, and ensuring security aligns with business objectives.
